## Genius art (made for WILDLINE)

`game/assets/genius/` (bodies, parts and cards) is built by `tools/blender/genius_kit.py`
in Blender and owned by the project. Placeholder quality: a proof of the pipeline in
`docs/art-bible.md`, section 8.

## Wild kit (made for WILDLINE)

`game/assets/wild/` (ground tiles, edge pieces, props and set pieces for the line, the river
ruin, the junction and the crossing) is built by `tools/blender/wild_kit.py` in Blender and
owned by the project. Flat-shaded low poly, no textures, materials from a small named palette.
Placeholder quality, as the genius kit: a proof of the outdoor pipeline in `docs/art-bible.md`.

## Beasts (made for WILDLINE, on the Quaternius wolf's rig)

`game/assets/beasts/` (cut-wolf, river-thing, ruin-stag, molted-hound, post-breaker, current-eel)
is built by `tools/blender/beast_kit.py` from the CC0 Quaternius wolf above: its skeleton and
animations, reshaped and given new parts in Blender. Owned by the project; the wolf's CC0
licence covers the base.

## People (made for WILDLINE: MakeHuman's body on the KayKit rig)

`game/assets/people/` (the twenty-six bodies of the thirteen homes) is built by
`tools/blender/body_kit.py` from two CC0 sources:

- **MakeHuman's base mesh, shape targets, game rig weights and UV masks**, by the MakeHuman
  team and community, www.makehumancommunity.org, as bundled with **MPFB** (the MakeHuman
  Plugin For Blender). The assets are CC0 1.0 (`tools/blender/makehuman/LICENSE.ASSETS.md`);
  none of MPFB's GPL source code is used. The source data the kit reads is kept in
  `tools/blender/makehuman/` (the base mesh, the macro and detail targets, the game rig and
  its weights).
- **KayKit's skeleton and seventy-six animations** (the Adventurers pack above), its bones
  moved onto the human's joints.

The dress, hair, headgear, eyes and brows are made for WILDLINE by the kit. Owned by the
project; the two CC0 licences cover the bases.

## Weapons (made for WILDLINE)

`game/assets/weapons/` (thirty-seven pieces: the cities' blades, guns, poles and bows, the
Academy disciplines' needles, dividers, brushes, ledgers and astrolabes, two shields and two
staves) is built by `tools/blender/weapon_kit.py` in Blender and owned by the project.
Flat-shaded low poly, no textures, materials from a small named palette; sized for the human
hand and hung from the hand slots on KayKit's axes. Nothing a person carries is KayKit's
any more.
