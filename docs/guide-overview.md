# WILDLINE — The Guide: Field, Genius, and Jobs

Nov 4, 2026 · @Zak

*How a character's will becomes a field, how the field becomes a companion that changes with them, and how the techniques they learn become the jobs the world remembers.*

## How the three fit

The field is the substance. Every living thing's will presses on the world as a field with a reserve, a reach, a density, a Shape, and a pulse, and everything a character does in a fight is that field held, shaped, projected, or patterned. The Field tab is the physics.

The genius is the field embodied. It is not a pet the character owns; it is what the character's will looks like when it stands up on its own, read from everything they are and everything they do. It grows by instars, adapts between them, specializes when the player chooses, hybridizes with what it fights beside, and changes form when the world forces it. The Genius tab is the biology.

The jobs are the field trained. A technique is one thing a field can be made to do; a Discipline is a city's way of teaching; an archetype is a bundle of techniques the world has seen enough times to name. Skills belong to the character; jobs are how the world recognizes them. The Skills to Jobs tab is the curriculum.

One rule ties them, and it is the rule the whole game is built to make true: **nothing about a character is a number they picked; everything is something they did.** The field's Shape is read at the rite. The genius's forms are read from play. The jobs are earned by witnessed work. The Pattern is a vow. A three-year character is a record, and the Three Lives tab shows three of them.

## Glossary

| Term | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Field | The physical pressure of a living thing's will; the medium of all magic |
| Will | The field's reserve, a bar of 100 spent on stances, techniques, and Patterns |
| Reach | How far past the skin the field extends; grows with Stage |
| Density | The field's output multiplier; ×1 to ×4 by Stage |
| Shape | The direction a field bends: Anchor, Turn, Cast, Read, Bind, Make |
| Pulse | The field's tempo, the beat techniques land on |
| Leak | An untrained or exhausted field spilling: beasts find it |
| Stage | The four levels of training: Hold, Shape, Project, Pattern |
| Still and Fold | Closing the field entirely; concentrating it in one part |
| Chord | Three party pulses in sync; a state that makes every beat perfect for a few beats |
| Technique | One thing a field can be made to do; the unit of skill |
| Family | Three groups of techniques per Shape |
| Discipline | A city's school: a weapon class, a stance modifier, three school techniques |
| Archetype | A job: Shape × Discipline, nine techniques, a stance, a name the world knows |
| Mark | A witnessed quest completed in an archetype; the unit of rank |
| Grade | A technique's level of mastery by use: learned, practiced, perfected |
| Pattern | The character's signature technique: Form plus Terms; the field's intention |
| Terms | Triggers, Tolls, Bars: the conditions that make a Pattern strong |
| Intention tier | The Pattern's growth by witnessed use, I to V |
| Genius | The field embodied as a companion |
| Expression | What kind of thing the genius is: Beast, Kith, Engine, Relic, Daemon, Aspect |
| Seed | The player's one-line leaning at creation; leaves the Seed-mark |
| Instar | One of the genius's six forms; an evolution |
| Reading | The evolution event: the Tally scored against the lattice; three candidates |
| Tally | The open record of play the Reading scores |
| Lattice | The library of forms: silhouettes, features, palettes |
| Feature | A visible part of a form: eyes, limbs, surfaces, voices, wings, shells |
| Heritage | Features carried from one form to the next |
| Adaptation | A small, reversible feature gained between instars from repeated play |
| Focus | A chosen specialization at the Fourth Form |
| Graft | A feature taken from another field by hybridization |
| Kin-form | A graft shared between two players' geniuses |
| Secondary form | A shift the genius can take once its condition is met |
| Shift | The order that changes the genius into a secondary form |
| Shadow | A pale copy of a witnessed Pattern the genius carries |
| Resonance | The higher of the genius's instar and the Pattern's tier; both draw on it |
| Accord | Instar 5 and tier V together; unlocks Apex and Union |
| Union | The genius and character merged for seven beats |
| Shade | A genius kept at the hearth after its partner is gone |
