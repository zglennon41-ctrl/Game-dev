# Systems

Fifteen systems, in reading order. The first six are the loop that writes the world; the next five are the logic under the fight; the last four are the fight, the work, the politics, and the money.

1. 1 Characters and Slots — three lives per server, alts and the first guild, retirement, the Sworn
2. 2 The Academy — every character's first act; cohorts, the rite, the Field Examination, Commencement
3. 3 Guilds — founding with classmates, Charters, halls and junctions, Atmosphere, tiers, Rallies
4. 4 Calendar and Seasons — one real day is one game day; the week, the month, the four seasons, the term
5. 5 Crisis Storylines — thirteen weeks, three Ledgers, the first term's twelve crises
6. 6 Server History — the Chronicle, how servers diverge, what a late cohort inherits, caravans
7. 7 Will, Shapes and Stages — the field, the six Shapes and their families, the four Stages, Still and Fold
8. 8 Terms and Patterns — the full grammar of 55 Terms, swearing, breach, the Gap
9. 9 Blood — Marks, the twelve kindreds, the six bloodlines, registration
10. 10 Genius and Shades — Seeds, six Expressions, instars read from how you play, secondary forms, Resonance with the Pattern, the fused, the hearth
11. 11 Archetypes and Founding — the 72 base archetypes, ranks, the build, founding and canon
12. 12 Combat — the pulse, the four verbs, weapons by city, beasts and breaks, PvP
13. 13 Quests and Excursions — the board, the seven quest types, the excursion, zones, the Quiet
14. 14 Houses, Charter and Compact — the five houses as factions, Favor, Writs, Writ wars, the Compact, the Convention
15. 15 Economy and Gear — four currencies, gear tiers, the Smith, trade, what is never for sale
