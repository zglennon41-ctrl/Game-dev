# The Academy

Every character begins at the Academy at Alexandria, the thirteenth fortress, and leaves it with a Shape, a Stage 3 field, a cohort, and a guild. The Academy is the tutorial, the first act, the server's recruiting floor, and the place a server's own archetypes get taught to the next generation.

## Solo and offline (owner, 2026-11-05)

The Academy is played alone and offline, before a character ever reaches a server. It is
where the field and the genius have time to become something no other player has, and
where the player learns the world, the fight and the systems. Where this file describes
players in a cohort, weekly enrollment, or Commencement at server times, read it as the
original online design, kept for reference until the owner decides what survives:

- The cohort is the twelve NPC classmates, generated new for every run.
- No one dies here. The wards keep training safe; on an excursion a rescue team is on
  standby, and a downed student is carried out. Failure costs the run (a retake, a
  classmate hurt, the cohort's opinion), never the character.
- Death is a server matter, rare and spectacular. After it, the next character begins
  again at the questionnaire and this Academy.
- The full length stands: 8–12 hours for a first character.

## The place

Alexandria is the fortress built around the Library, and the Academy is its upper city: dormitories by Shape, the quad, the yard, the hearth hall where the rite is held, the Instructors' tower, and the Gate, the platform where graduates leave on the line. It is the only fortress every city sends its awakened to, which is why every city's Discipline has a teacher here and why the cohort you graduate with is the most mixed room you will ever stand in.

## Enrollment and cohorts

A cohort is everyone who enrolled on the server in the same week, Monday lamp hour to Monday lamp hour, plus twelve NPC classmates generated for that cohort with names, bloods, Shapes, and lives. A cohort of players can be three or three hundred; the NPC twelve are always there, so a solo player has classmates and a founding is always possible.

Cohorts have a name (the Academy numbers them: the Hundred-and-Fourth Cohort) and the Chronicle records who was in each one. Cohort-mates get a lifelong tag the hall can see, and a shade accepts a cohort-mate of its partner faster than a stranger.

## The curriculum

The Academy is eight to twelve hours of play for a first character, paced as a term of six lessons. Real time does not gate lessons; it gates Commencement, below.

| Lesson | What you learn | What proves it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hold | Containing the field; the guard and the parry; why beasts find leaking children | A yard drill against an Instructor's genius |
| 2. The rite | The hearth bowl finds your Shape; the genius takes its First Form, read from the Seed, your blood, and your Shape | The rite itself, with the cohort watching |
| 3. Shape | The stance; your Shape's first technique family | A sparring ladder within the cohort |
| 4. Discipline | Your city's school; first weapon; the Discipline's first three techniques | The Instructor's test |
| 5. The line | The car, the post, extraction, orders for the genius, the party of three | A Green excursion with two classmates: the Field Examination |
| 6. Project | Stage 3: extending the field past the skin | Passing the Field Examination with your field open; at the Gate the genius takes its Second Form, read from how you played the Academy |

Between lessons the Academy is a place to live: the quad has the card game, the yard has pick-up sparring, the Instructors have stories, and the classmates have problems that turn into side quests. The cohort's NPC twelve are written like roster members: one will found with you if you ask, one will refuse, one will die in the Field Examination if the party fails it.

## The Field Examination

The exam is a real Green excursion, a party of three from the cohort (players or NPC classmates), with an Instructor watching from the post. Pass and you are Stage 3. Fail and you retake it at the next lamp hour with a different zone; a second failure means a classmate was hurt, and the cohort remembers. The exam is also the first witnessed quest in your record, and the first time a named NPC can die because of you.

## Commencement

Commencement is held every night at 20:00 server time and the Grand Commencement every Sunday at 18:00. A graduate may leave at any nightly Commencement. The Grand Commencement is where guilds recruit in force: every guild with standing sends a recruiter, their offers stand on the board, and founders from the cohort declare.

At Commencement a graduate does one of three things:

| Choice | What it needs | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join a guild | An offer on the board, accepted by both sides | A hall, a hearth, a roster, a guild mark placed that night |
| Found a guild with classmates | Three graduates of the cohort from three accounts, NPC classmates filling in; a name; a Charter template | A Company with a Writ of Founding, lodging at a chartered guild's hall or a city hall until it wins a junction |
| Leave as a Free Lance | Nothing | Quests from city boards only, no hearth, no Rally; recruitable at any later Commencement |

Guilds recruiting at Commencement see what the Academy saw: Shape, blood, Discipline, exam result, and the cohort's own write-up of you, which the NPC classmates contribute to and players can add to. A guild can make an offer conditional on a Term.

## Advanced standing

Second and third characters enter with advanced standing: lessons 1, 3, 4 and 6 are compressed into a two-hour refresher, but the rite and the Field Examination are never skipped, because the Shape is new and the cohort is new. Advanced standing takes three to four hours, and the character graduates in a current cohort, with current classmates, into the server as it is now.

## What the Academy teaches on an old server

The Academy's archetype lessons come from the server's canon, so on a three-year-old server lesson 4 offers founded archetypes beside the base ones, taught by the Instructors who hold them. The Instructors' tower is where graduates return: a Master-rank character can take an Instructor's seat for a season, which puts their founded archetype on the curriculum and their name on every cohort that learns it. Instructor seats are a Charter Convention matter; the first term has twelve.
