# Calendar and Seasons

One real day is one game day, and the world keeps its appointments whether you are there or not. The calendar gives the server a shared rhythm (the board in the morning, the Rally on Friday, Commencement on Sunday, the crisis every season) without ever gating a player's own progress on the clock.

## The day

The day turns at 04:00 server time, the lamp hour, when the hall relights its lamps and the board refreshes. Night on the line is real night: Black lines run only between dusk and the lamp hour, beast behavior shifts after dark, and the hall's common room fills in the evening because that is when people are home. Nothing is lost by missing a day; the board's postings carry over for 48 hours before they expire.

## The week

| Day | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Rumor day: the week's long postings go up; house notices; the cohort closes and a new one opens at the Academy |
| Wednesday | Compact day: Linework contracts, line status, Allotment business in the Thaw |
| Friday | Rally night, 20:00: guild matches in the Compact tunnels |
| Saturday | Market day: city traders at the Mountain Dock, the week's best gear |
| Sunday | The hearth: rites, swearing Terms, naming the dead; Grand Commencement at 18:00 |
| Every night | Commencement at 20:00 |

## The month

The first of the month is the Compact's posting day: standings published, tolls settled, tenancy due. The new moon opens the Quiet for three nights, the only time the Black story zones can be entered. The full moon is the beasts' night: fourth-molt beasts walk closer to the lines, and Hunts pay double.

## The four seasons

Seasons turn on the equinoxes and solstices, thirteen weeks each, and each one has a job in the server's year.

| Season | Starts | What it is for | Crisis shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thaw | March 20 | Lines reopen; the Allotment awards dark junctions; Charters may change; Companies become Chartered | The wild wakes: a threat on the lines |
| Writ | June 21 | The houses' season: Writs flood the boards, feuds go hot, Writ wars are legal, the Charter seat is contested | A political crisis among the houses |
| Rally | September 22 | The guild games: Friday Rallies count double, junctions can change hands, Standing is made | A crisis of guilds against guilds, or all of them against something |
| Dark | December 21 | The wild pushes in; hall fields are tested; Deep Runs; the year's dead are named at Midwinter | A survival crisis; in the third year, the Charter Convention |

The season turn is a server event: the crisis Reckoning resolves at the lamp hour on the turn day, the world changes, and the Chronicle writes the season's page.

## The Charter term

Three years, twelve seasons, twelve crises. The term is the unit of history: the first Charter is the one the game ships with, and the Charter Convention at the third Dark lets the server's Seat guilds and surviving houses amend it. Amendments are real rule changes within a bounded list (the Houses tab has it), and the next term starts under the amended Charter. A term is also when a server is re-advertised to new players as what it has become.

## What real time gates, and what it never does

| Gated by the clock | Never gated |
| --- | --- |
| The Rally (Friday), Commencement (nightly, Grand on Sunday), the Allotment (Thaw), the Quiet (new moon) | Lessons, Stages, Marks, archetype ranks, gear, any personal progression |
| Crisis stages and Reckonings | Quests; the board always has work |
| Standing decay (seasonal) and genius life stages (days since settling) | A character's power; a player who plays twice as much is not slower |
| House relations shifting with the season | Access to any zone below Black |

The rule for designers: real time shapes the world, never the player. A player who logs in once a month should find the world moved and their character where they left it.

## Time zones

Each server has a home time zone named in its listing, and all clock events are in that zone. Players choose a server by region; the game recommends the one whose Rally night lands in their evening.
