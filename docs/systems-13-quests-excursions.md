# Quests and Excursions

The board is where the world asks the guild for something, and the excursion is the guild answering. Every run is 20 to 40 minutes from platform to hearth, every posting says who could die, and what comes home is loot, Marks, and a changed hall.

## The board

Each hall's board refreshes at the lamp hour with 9 to 15 postings, drawn from the server's state: the Compact's Linework for the lines the hall touches, Hunts from nearby towns, Writs from houses that know the guild, Salvage from the Archive's wants, crisis-tagged work in season. Postings live 48 hours. A posting shows the line color, the poster, the pay, the crisis Ledger it serves if any, and the named members at risk: the NPC roster members the quest will draw into the zone, who can die if it fails.

Company guilds without a hall read a city board, which has no Writs, no Deep Runs, and no named risk, because a Company has nobody to lose yet.

## The seven types

| Type | Poster | Structure | Fails if | Pays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Linework | The Compact | Reach a tunnel section, clear it, restore the resonance post by holding it three beats with a Make or an Anchor | The post is not restored by extraction | Standing, Notes; the line stays lit |
| Salvage | The Archive, the cities | Reach a ruin, find the thing, carry it out; the carrier cannot use Patterns | The thing is dropped and not recovered | Gear, archetype fragments (a technique's first third), lore pages |
| Hunt | Towns, houses | A named beast, on a timer; the beast remembers parties that flee | The beast escapes or kills a named member | Materials, genius Witness, a trophy for the hall |
| Escort | The Compact, houses | A train or airship through a zone; the vehicle has health; waves come from both sides | The vehicle falls below a quarter | Notes, Favor with the owner |
| Writ | A house | Political: retrieve a person or thing, arrest, protect, embarrass, disappear; a decision timer by Charter | The house's terms are not met | Favor up with the house, down with its rival; Notes; crisis weight in Writ season |
| Deep Run | The guild | Multi-stage: two zones in sequence with one extraction, into the deep wild or the Quiet; Red at least | Any stage fails | The best gear, story flags, relit junctions in the Thaw |
| Rally | The Compact | Three against three; objective; Fridays | Lose the match | Standing, prestige, junctions in Rally season |

## The excursion

| Step | Time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform | 1–3 min | Gear, consumables, build, genius orders; the party forms; the posting's named members join as AI if they are drawn |
| The car | 60–90 s | Ride out; party dialogue; the named members speak; the Instructor's or Master's brief; nothing to skip the first time, skippable after |
| Areas 1–2 | 6–12 min | Tiles with set pieces; beasts of the zone; the first objective |
| The post | 1–2 min | Checkpoint, regroup, Will and health restored by half, the choice: push to the boss or extract with what you carry |
| Areas 3–5 | 8–16 min | The zone's heart; the boss or the objective; named members at risk here |
| Extraction | 60 s | The ride home; the loot and Marks tally; who is being carried |
| The hearth | 2–5 min | Witness marks logged, genius growth shown, loot sorted, the roster's reactions, the board's response |

Extracting at the post keeps everything carried so far and fails the quest's objective, which is sometimes the right call: a Hunt with a named member down is a quest to abandon.

## Zones from tiles

A zone is a territory's tile library (12 to 20 tiles per territory at launch) assembled into 3 to 5 areas with fixed set pieces: the bridge-thing is always at the end of the Shenandoah Ruin, the aerie's ledge is always the third area of Blue Ridge. Seeds vary tile order, beast placement, weather, and which of the zone's three variants is active, and variants are written to the server's state, so a zone on a server where the Cahokia line went dark has a dark-line variant others never see.

## Loot and Marks

Loot is rolled per party and shared by need: gear drops are rolled against each member's archetype affinity, materials split, Notes to the account wallet with the guild's cut. Marks are personal and need a witness. Salvage fragments assemble into techniques at the Archive, three fragments to a technique. Every quest also pays the hall: Standing for the Compact's work, Atmosphere for a quest where nobody was lost, Favor for a house's.

## The Quiet

The Quiet is the place with no field. Inside it Will does not regenerate, geniuses are silent and will not take orders, Read sees nothing, and the pulse is gone, so every input is off-beat unless the character goes Still and counts the beats themselves. The Quiet opens on the new moon for three nights, holds the story's Black zones, and moves when a crisis says it does. Nothing from the Quiet can be carried out except by a character who never used a Pattern inside it.
