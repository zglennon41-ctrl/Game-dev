# Genius and Shades

A genius is the character's field made into a companion, and it is read, not chosen: at every evolution the game reads how the character has actually played and gives the genius a new base form that carries a few features of the old one. The Pattern is the field's intention and the genius is the field itself, and Resonance binds them so that growing either one grows both.

## Two reflections

|  | The genius | The Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is | The field: everything the character is | The intention: what the character means to do with it |
| Shaped by | Seed, blood, Shape, Discipline, how and where they fight, who they fight beside, the hall, the dead they have seen, their Terms | The Form they chose and the Terms they accepted |
| Grows by | Instars: evolutions read from play | Intention tiers: witnessed use |
| Changes | Its base form at every instar; secondary forms at milestones | Its length and payout at every tier |
| Coupled by | Resonance: an evolution strengthens the Pattern; a tier gained evolves the genius |  |

## What a genius can be

Six Expressions. A genius can be any of them, and the Reading can carry it from one to another when the play says so.

| Expression | What it is | How it fights | Leans from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beast | An animal, grown strange | Claws, speed, the flank | Hunts, the wild, Cast and Anchor play |
| Kith | A small humanoid: a fey thing, a homunculus, a child of the field with a face | Weapons of its own, a voice, tricks | Bind and Read play, the hall, bonds with members |
| Engine | Sentient technology: a walking lantern, a clockwork hound, a gun that thinks | Steady output, wards, the post | Colonia and Lunden gear, Make play, Linework |
| Relic | Sentient equipment, worn or wielded: a coat, a blade, a mask, a gauntlet | Lives on the character; fights as part of them | Close fighting, Fold, Edo and Columbia weapons |
| Daemon | A demon or spirit with a contract and a want | Contracts, fear, the Gap's edge | Demon blood, Bind Contract, Writs, sworn Terms |
| Aspect | The field with no body: a flame, a shadow, a chord, a weather | Surrounds the character; shapes the ground | Turn play, Ascetic Discipline, the Quiet, Aspect Seeds |

## The Seed

At creation the player does not pick a body. They pick a Seed: one of twenty-four leanings in plain words, and the Seed weights the first Reading and leaves one feature, the Seed-mark, that every later form carries.

| Seed | Leans toward |
| --- | --- |
| Something that watches from above | Beast or Kith with wings; eyes that see far |
| Something that stands between me and harm | Engine or Beast, broad; a wall |
| Something I can hold | Relic |
| Something that was never alive | Engine or Aspect |
| Something with a voice | Kith or Daemon |
| Something that burns | Aspect or Daemon; Turn |
| Something that remembers | Kith; a Read lean; shades accept it sooner |
| Something that owes me | Daemon; a contract from day one |

The other sixteen are in the same register. A Seed is a sentence about what the player wants beside them, and the game keeps that promise in one feature for the genius's whole life.

## Instars

A genius has six forms over a life, and every instar is a new base form. Evolutions are unlocked by milestones, not by the clock; the minimum days exist only so a genius cannot be rushed through its life in a week.

| Instar | Called | Unlocked by | Minimum days | What is read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First Form | The rite | 0 | Seed, blood, Shape |
| 2 | Second Form | The Gate: passing the Field Examination | 7 | Academy play: lessons leaned on, the exam party, orders given |
| 3 | Third Form | The first Pattern built, or Intention tier II | 30 | The Pattern's Form and Terms, the Discipline most used, the guild joined |
| 4 | Fourth Form | Adept in any archetype, or Intention tier III | 90 | Range of fighting, technique families, zones, who you fight beside |
| 5 | Fifth Form | Master in any archetype, or Intention tier IV | 365 | Everything since, weighted toward the last season; the hall; the dead |
| 6 | Apex | Accord (below) | 540 | The whole life |

## The Reading

When an instar unlocks, the genius reads the Tally: the record of play since the last Reading. The Tally is kept in the open, on the genius's page, so a player can see what their genius is learning about them.

| The Tally tracks | How |
| --- | --- |
| Technique families used, by Shape | Count and share |
| Range | Distance at which hits land: close, mid, far |
| Tempo | Weapon classes and the pulse they set |
| Orders | Which orders the genius was given most |
| Company | The Shapes and Expressions of the party members fought beside |
| Ground | Zones run: tunnel, ruin, water, sky, the Quiet |
| Intention | The Pattern's Form and Terms, and how often it fires |
| Bonds | Shades spoken with, members fought beside most, Rallies won |
| Blood and hall | The character's blood; the hall's Atmosphere and its Expression mix |

The Reading scores every form in the lattice by fit against the Tally and offers the top three. Each candidate shows its Expression, the heritage features it carries from the current form, and the lines of the Tally that chose it. The player picks one, or lets it settle on the best fit. A Reading can cross Expressions: a Beast can become a Relic if the character fights close and keeps it at hand.

A worked example. A player seeds something that watches from above, rolls a Cast Shape, and the First Form is a hawk. From the Academy on they fight close, with a saber, and take Hold and Guard orders. At the Gate the Reading offers: a Harrier, a ground-hunting Beast with the hawk's eyes and a crest; a Stooping Kith, a hawk-headed small warrior with a wing-cloak and a blade; and a Talon Relic, a brass gauntlet that opens into a hawk's foot. The Seed-mark (eyes that see far) is on all three. The hawk is gone, and the player's play is standing in its place.

## The lattice

Forms are compositions, not hand-built bodies: a silhouette (six per Expression, thirty-six at launch), three features from a library of about sixty (eyes, limbs, surfaces, voices, lights, wings, shells), and the Shape variant's palette and proportions. One hundred twenty named forms ship at launch and thousands of visible variants fall out of the features. Heritage features are literally the same parts carried forward. This is the only way a base form can change at every instar across six Expressions without a studio of a hundred animators, and it is the production risk the Building It section of the bible should name first.

## Secondary forms

From the Third Form a genius can develop up to three secondary forms, each unlocked by a condition the Tally can see and shifted into for Will. A secondary form keeps the base form's features and changes what the genius is for.

| Secondary form | Condition | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mount | Rider Adept, and a Beast or Engine form | Carries the character; Cast Speed from its back |
| Arms | Relic or Engine form, and 50 Hunts beside the character | Becomes the character's weapon for a fight; Fold costs half |
| Ward | 100 Make Ward uses in its field | Stands as a post: a party can regroup inside it |
| Night | 50 runs after dark | Still with the genius; Black lines a shade brighter |
| Storm | 200 Turn Element uses in its field | Carries the character's last element and lays it on the ground |
| Herald | Kith or Daemon, and a Bind Contract Pattern | Speaks a Bar aloud: a foe covered by it hesitates a beat |
| Shade-walker | A shade's acceptance | Carries that shade's one Pattern as a shadow at ×2 |
| War | A Rally season won | A form for the tunnels: objective carry, no Hunt |
| Carrier | 30 Escorts | Holds a Salvage carry so the character keeps their Pattern |
| Union | Apex, and Accord | The genius and the character merge for seven beats: the fused's body, briefly, with every shadow available |

## Orders

| Order | The genius | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guard | Stays at the partner's side, blocks the hit the partner did not see | First Form |
| Hunt | Focuses the partner's lock-on target with its own family's techniques | First Form |
| Hold | Takes and keeps a position or a post | Second Form |
| Free | Its own judgment: a model of the partner's last thirty days of play, visible on its page | Second Form |
| Duet | Joins the Pattern: the chain doubles and the payout with it | Fourth Form |
| Shift | Changes into a secondary form, 20 Will | Third Form |

The genius's Will is 40 at the First Form and rises ten per instar to 90 at Apex. It has no health bar; it falls when its Will is emptied by damage.

## Witness and shadows

A genius witnesses a Pattern used inside its field, about ten paces, by anyone. Ten witnessed uses make a shadow, a copy at ×1 with the Triggers intact and the Tolls absent, used on Hunt. Shadows are how a genius carries the dead. The cap is one shadow at the Second Form, two at the Third, three from the Fourth, and an Apex genius can teach its shadows at the hearth while its partner is alive. Shadows can be forgotten on purpose at the hearth, which Toll 18 does by force.

## Resonance: field and intention

The Pattern has Intention tiers, earned by witnessed use, the way the genius has instars.

| Tier | Needs | The Pattern gains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Built | The chain as the Terms tab describes it |
| II | 50 witnessed uses | A verse: one more beat, and the payout with it |
| III | 150 | A second verse |
| IV | 400, and Stage 4 for a year | The Pattern can be fired from Still |
| V | 400, sworn, and Master in the archetype it was built in | A third verse; the chain can be split across two beats of silence |

Resonance is the higher of the genius's instar and the Pattern's tier, and both sides draw on it. The genius fights at Resonance, not at its instar: its Will, its shadow cap, and its output scale with the higher number, so a character who fires their Pattern four hundred times has a genius that fights like a Fifth Form even if it has not evolved. The Pattern pays at Resonance, not at its tier: a character who has bonded, witnessed, and shifted their genius to the Fifth Form has a Pattern with two verses even if they rarely fire it.

And each side's milestone unlocks the other's next step: an evolution grants the Pattern its next tier's verse; a tier gained unlocks the genius's next Reading (the minimum days still hold). Nobody loses the side they neglected. What differs is the shape: a genius evolved mostly by Pattern use is read from the Intention line of the Tally, so it takes forms that look like the Pattern's Terms, a Daemon for a character with cruel Tolls, an Aspect for one whose Trigger is the night. The spammer's genius becomes a portrait of the spamming, which is the system working.

Accord is instar 5 and tier V together. It unlocks Apex and Union, and it is the condition the Chronicle records when it names a character great.

## The fused

A fused character is their own genius: the base form is their body, the Trunk is the beast's, and the Readings change them. They are in Union permanently, which is their power and their Gap: no separate orders, no shadows, no Duet, no shade, and the hearth cannot hear them. Their Resonance is the Pattern's tier alone, so a fused character's road is intention. They earn Marks at 1.5× because every fight is theirs alone.

## Falling and recovery

A fallen genius lies where it fell until the post or extraction, then recovers at the hearth at the lamp hour. Three falls in a season cost the genius its newest secondary form; an Apex genius cannot fall more than once a run.

## Shades

When a character dies (Sworn) or retires, the genius goes to the guild's hearth as a shade in its final form, keeping every shadow it carried and its partner's Pattern with the Terms the partner swore. It teaches to members it accepts, and acceptance is slow and social.

| Who | Wait before a shade will teach |
| --- | --- |
| A stranger | A season |
| A member of the same guild | A month |
| A cohort-mate of the dead | Two weeks |
| Kin, another character of the dead's account | One week |
| Someone who used a Bar against the dead | Never |

Shades speak at the hearth on Sundays, judge successions and Rally rosters, and go silent when the hall's field fails. A dark hall's shades are lost unless the junction is relit within a season; a dissolved guild's go to the Academy hearth. A hearth holds twelve; the thirteenth pushes the oldest to the Academy's. A First-Form shade fades in a week.
