# Three Lives

Three characters, three accounts, one guild, one Charter term. Each life runs the field, the genius, and the jobs through three years of play, and the point of the tab is that none of the three ends up where their first hour said they would.

## The three

|  | Mira | Tobiah | Sable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blood | Mark: long stride | Kindred: Ridgefolk, stone-skinned | Bloodline: demon-blooded, House Key |
| Seed | Something that watches from above | Something that stands between me and harm | Something that owes me |
| Shape at the rite | Cast | Anchor | Bind |
| City | Columbia (Marshal) | Columbia (Marshal) | Lunden (Aeronaut) |
| First Form | A hawk (Beast) | A broad iron hound (Engine) | A small horned figure with a ledger (Daemon) |
| Player's habit | Says ranged, fights close | Fires the Pattern constantly, barely orders the genius | Bonds with everything, plays every Sunday at the hearth |

They graduate in the same cohort, found a guild called the Lamplit Company with two NPC classmates, and win a dark junction at the second Thaw.

## Year one

**Mira.** Stage 3 at the Gate with a saber in her hand and Guard orders on her tongue; the Reading offers a Harrier, a Stooping Kith, a Talon Relic, and she takes the Kith with the hawk's eyes and wings for a cloak. She builds her Pattern in the Writ season, Stoop (Cast Reach, a closing strike; Trigger after a perfect Hold, Toll the genius sits out, Bar never against the unarmed), and the Third Form reads the Pattern and the Marshal school and gives her a Hawk-Mask Relic she wears. She is Adept Deadeye by the Rally, with a cross coming: she has been learning Edo Draw-Cut from a shade. Resonance 3: the Pattern at tier II, the genius at the Third Form.

**Tobiah.** He fires Bulwark Stand (Anchor Wall, cannot be moved; Trigger only with a member behind him, Toll half his health) in every fight, two hundred times by midsummer, and reaches Intention tier III before his genius has evolved past the Second Form. Resonance says the iron hound fights like a Fourth Form anyway, and the tier-III milestone unlocks a Reading he did not earn by bonding; the Reading, weighted by the Intention line of the Tally, offers an Engine with a shield-face, a Relic tower-shield that is also a hound, and a Daemon that stands behind him. He takes the tower-shield without reading the candidates. His genius now looks like his Pattern. He is Adept Bulwark with a stone-skinned Carroll variant's cousin on the list, because the Ridgefolk Gift (sinks in water) is also his Gap, and the Lamplit Company's zones are dry.

**Sable.** Bind, demon-blooded, with the Trunk's Contract from day one and a genius that is a contract with a face. She fires her Pattern (Collection: Bind Contract, the foe pays a Toll of Sable's choosing; Trigger only after naming the foe aloud, Toll a point of Favor with House Key, Bar never against her cohort) rarely, but she is at the hearth every Sunday, and by autumn two shades accept her. Her Third Form grafts a shade's feature (a lantern-jaw from a dead Archivist's Kith) and her genius gains Shade-walker. Resonance 3 from the genius side, and her Pattern has a verse she has not earned by use.

## Year two

**Mira.** Adept Draw-Cutter: a cross title, Deadeye Draw-Cutter, across one Shape. Her Fourth Form is read from a season of Hunts at close range with an Edo blade: Hawk-Mask, Edged, with Focus Warden and the secondary form Arms, so the mask becomes a blade-hawk for a fight. She has run a hundred lines beside Tobiah and their geniuses take a Kin-form: her mask grows a shield-brow, his shield grows a hawk's eye, and the two geniuses can Chorus. In the Registration crisis the Lamplit Company declares for the guild exemption, which is Sable's fight, and Mira runs the Red zones that carry the weight.

**Tobiah.** Four hundred uses. Tier IV: Bulwark Stand can be fired from Still, which he has never learned. The tier unlocks the Fifth Form's Reading at day 365, and the Tally is almost all intention: the candidates are a Relic bastion, a Daemon that is a wall with a mouth, and an Aspect, a standing weight of air. He takes the bastion, and the Chronicle's first mention of him is as a Master Bulwark whose genius is a door. He has no secondary forms except Ward, which the Make Wards of a party-mate earned for him, and his genius has two adaptations (plated, set feet) and no grafts but the Kin-form Mira forced on him by standing beside him. He is strong, and his genius is a portrait of standing still.

**Sable.** Master Clerk (Bind Examiner) after the rite of the school, an examination in Jiangnan's quarter of Lunden; the Examiner's Office modifier makes her Contracts last a beat longer, which with the demon Trunk's Contract is the strongest Bind on the line. Fourth Form by the Adept milestone: a Daemon with a second face (her own blood's graft) and Focus Voice, so her Duet chain has an extra beat. She fires Collection eighty times all year and does not care; her Resonance is 4 from the genius, and her Pattern pays with two verses she earned by bonding. In the Key Heir crisis, House Key's Writ names Dashiell, and Sable, Favor 70 with Key and a Bar against her cohort, takes the Writ and finds Dashiell was in her cohort's year. She refuses it at the post. Favor −30. The hall's Atmosphere rises five.

## Year three

**Mira.** Master Draw-Cutter with Stoop built in it, which makes her Pattern tier V when sworn, and she swears it at the hearth in the Thaw with Tobiah and Sable as witnesses. Instar 5 and tier V: Accord. Her Fifth Form read the Reef run (a Quiet place on the genius) and a Colonia Engine shade's clockwork wings; her Sixth is Apex, a falcon-harness with Pressure, and in the Hearth War she takes the Lamplit Company's Rally final in Union. She founds: Stoop, used by Mira, Tobiah's second character, and an NPC classmate, becomes a provisional in the Rally and canon at the Dark, with her name on it. On the next server's caravan it is a job strangers swear.

**Tobiah.** Tier V needs a sworn Pattern and Master rank, and he has both, so Accord comes from the Pattern side, which the hearth has never seen: a genius that reaches Apex having been ordered perhaps fifty times in three years. The shades argue about it on a Sunday. His Apex form is the bastion opened: a Relic that is a gate, with the hawk's eye from the Kin-form and nothing else of anyone. He retires at the Convention, and his shade teaches Bulwark Stand to anyone who asks, at ×9, with the Terms he swore.

**Sable.** Her genius reaches the Fifth Form in the Writ season of year three and Apex needs Accord, which needs tier V, which needs four hundred uses, and she has two hundred. So she fires Collection two hundred times in the Hearth War, naming every foe aloud, paying Key a point each time until her Favor is −100 and House Key hires against her. Accord in the Dark. Her Apex form is the Daemon with the ledger closed, and the Convention makes the Lamplit Company a Seat guild, and Sable, demon-blooded and a Master, petitions for House Sable, which is on the bounded list, and which the Convention passes.

## What the three lives show

| Question | Answer in play |
| --- | --- |
| Does an early choice trap you? | Mira's hawk became a mask, a blade, a harness; the Seed-mark is the only thing that stayed |
| Does neglecting the genius cost you? | Tobiah's genius fought at Fifth-Form strength from year two; what it cost him was a body that looked like a door |
| Does neglecting the Pattern cost you? | Sable's Pattern had two verses before she had fired it a hundred times; what it cost her was four hundred uses in one season when she wanted Accord |
| Do jobs follow play? | A Marshal's daughter is a Draw-Cutter; a Bind is a Clerk because Lunden had an examination hall |
| Does the world remember? | Stoop is canon, Bulwark Stand is a shade's lesson, and there is a House Sable |
