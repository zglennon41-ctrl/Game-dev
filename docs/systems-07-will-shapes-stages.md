# Will, Shapes and Stages

A living thing's will is a field, the field has one of six Shapes, and training it runs through four Stages. Everything a character can do in a fight is this system; the Terms, Blood, Genius, and Archetype tabs all hang off it.

## Will

Will is the field's reserve, shown as a bar beside health. It is spent by Shape stances, techniques, and Patterns, refunded by perfect Holds, and regenerated at 4 points a second with the field open, 0 when Still. The bar is 100 for everyone; what grows is not the bar but what a point buys, because each Stage multiplies the field's output. An untrained field leaks, which means beasts can smell you from twice the distance; Hold fixes it, which is why it is lesson one.

## The rite

The hearth bowl is a stone bowl of still water on the hearthstone. A character holds it, the cohort watches, and the water answers in one of six ways. The answer is weighted by blood (a kindred leans toward its usual Shape, a bloodline more so) and by nothing the player chooses, so a cohort of Anchors is possible and the Academy's dormitories are sized for it. The rite can be retaken once per character in the Writ season; the second answer stands.

## The six Shapes and their families

Each Shape has three technique families, nine techniques each at launch, learned from Instructors, masters in the yard, shades, and archetypes.

| Shape | Family 1 | Family 2 | Family 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anchor | Wall (guards, bracing, immovability) | Weight (heavy strikes, ground, staggers) | Endurance (recovery, carrying, holding a post) |
| Turn | Element (fire, cold, current, rot) | Property (hardening, softening, lightening) | Conversion (turning one thing into another: a wound into heat, a shot into mist) |
| Cast | Shot (projectiles from the field) | Reach (extending strikes, pull, push) | Speed (bursts, blinks, the field thrown forward with the body behind it) |
| Read | Sense (seeing fields, the break, through walls) | Foresight (the next beat, the next move) | Mending (setting a field right: healing, cleansing, waking a fallen genius) |
| Bind | Leash (slowing, holding, pulling) | Puppet (moving another body for a beat) | Contract (debuffs that cost the target something specific) |
| Make | Form (conjured weapons and tools) | Ward (constructs that stand: walls, posts, traps) | Summon (a construct that acts for a few beats) |

## Affinity

The wheel runs Anchor, Turn, Cast, Read, Bind, Make. A character learns and uses techniques of any Shape, at the wheel's discount.

| Distance | Affinity | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Own Shape | 100% | Full output; all three families; Patterns |
| Adjacent | 80% | Two families; techniques cost 25% more Will |
| Two away | 60% | One family; techniques cost 50% more Will |
| Opposite | 40% | One technique per family; costs double; never a Pattern |

Beasts have Shapes, and the counter is the opposite: a Bind-shaped beast breaks from Turn, a Make from Cast, an Anchor from Read. A break is a stagger window of three beats.

## The four Stages

| Stage | Name | Learned | Needs | Field output | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hold | Academy lesson 1 | Nothing | ×1 | Guard, parry, no leaking |
| 2 | Shape | Academy lesson 3 | The rite | ×2 | The stance; family 1 |
| 3 | Project | Academy lesson 6, the Field Examination | Pass the exam | ×3 | Techniques beyond the skin; families 2 and 3; Journeyman archetypes |
| 4 | Pattern | A guild hearth | 10 witnessed quests and a Term | ×4 on the Pattern | The signature; swearing; Adept archetypes |

Stages are the only vertical progression in the game, and there are four. After Stage 4, a character grows horizontally: techniques, archetypes, Marks, Terms, gear, and the genius.

## Still and Fold

Still closes the field entirely. The character is invisible to beasts and to Read, regenerates no Will, cannot use techniques, and loses the genius's orders (it waits where it stood). Still is stealth, ambush, and the only way to cross the Quiet without going mad. It is learned from a Varanasi Ascetic or a shade who knew it.

Fold presses the whole field into one part of the body for up to three beats: a fist, a shoulder, an eye. A Folded strike multiplies by the Stage; a Folded guard takes a boss hit without a break. Fold costs 40 Will and leaves the rest of the body leaking for three beats after. It is learned from an Edo Blade or an Anchor Master.
