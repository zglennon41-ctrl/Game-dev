# Archetypes and Founding

An archetype is a job the world remembers: a Shape crossed with a Discipline, with a stance, a technique list, and a name any tavern on the continent knows. The game ships with 72, bloodline variants multiply them, and players found the rest, so that a server's job list is its own history.

## What an archetype is

| Part | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Shape and Discipline | The cell on the grid below; the archetype's identity |
| Stance | A Shape stance tuned by the Discipline: a Marshal's Cast stance is a shooting stance, an Aeronaut's is a falling one |
| Technique list | Nine techniques, three per rank, drawn from the Shape's families and the Discipline's weapon |
| Gear affinity | The weapon class and city genre the archetype is built around |
| Mastery technique | One technique only a Master has, teachable by a Master |
| Founder | The city tradition for the base 72; a player's name for the founded ones |

At launch the three reachable cities' Disciplines (Marshal, Pilot, Aeronaut) are taught at the Academy and in halls; the other nine are taught by Instructors from those cities who arrive with the airship routes and by shades who knew them. A Discipline outside your own Shape's affinity is still open: a Turn can be a Deadeye at 80%.

## Bloodline variants

A bloodline character in an archetype is a variant with the house's name on it and a bonus technique from the Trunk folded into the list: a dragon-blooded Bulwark is a Carroll Bulwark, and her Scale is on the archetype's list. Six lines by 72 is 432 variants, none of which need separate authoring beyond the Trunk.

## Ranks and Marks

A Mark is a quest completed with the archetype equipped and at least one party member present to witness it. Green lines give 1 Mark, Amber 2, Red 3, Black 5; Sworn characters double it.

| Rank | Needs | Gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Journeyman | Stage 3; take it at an Archive | Stance; techniques 1–3; gear affinity |
| Adept | 10 Marks in it; Stage 4 | Techniques 4–6; the archetype's name on the character |
| Master | 50 Marks in it and a Pattern built in it | Techniques 7–9; the mastery technique; may teach; may take an Instructor seat |

A character can hold any number of archetypes and switches at the hall. Techniques learned come with you, so a three-year character has a wide list and a build problem.

## The build

Eight slots, set at the hall or the car.

| Slot | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The Pattern |
| 2–5 | Four techniques from any archetype held, at the wheel's affinity |
| 6–7 | Two stances; switching stances in a fight costs a beat |
| 8 | The Duet, or empty until the genius reaches its Fourth Form |

The build is the Guild Wars half of the game: the archetype grid is the library, the build is the deck, and the pulse is the hand.

## Founding

Three members of one guild, from three accounts, each with 10 witnessed uses of a Pattern with the identical Form and Terms, may found. The guild's Archivist files the founding at the hall's archive with a name and a one-line description; the archetype is provisional from that moment, taking its technique list from the founders' shared build.

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Provisional | Listed in the Archive with the founders' names; the guild's members may take it; it counts for Marks |
| Canonization | At the next season turn, if the three founders are still members in good standing and the Pattern has 100 witnessed uses on the server, Timbuktu canonizes it; otherwise it stays provisional one more season, then lapses |
| Canon | Any Stage 4 character on the server may take it at any Archive by accepting its Terms; if the founders swore, so must the taker; the founders' names are on it forever, and the founding guild's |
| Taught | A Master of a canon archetype may take an Instructor seat; the Academy then offers it in lesson 4 |
| Carried | Caravans may send it to other servers as a provisional |

A server canonizes at most twelve archetypes a season, highest witnessed-use first, so canon is scarce and a founding is an event. A provisional whose Form and Terms match an existing archetype merges into it, with the new founders listed as refounders. Names pass a filter and the Archivist's word; the Compact can strike a name at a crisis ending.

## What founding does to the world

Founded archetypes are the clearest line between servers. By year three a server holds forty to a hundred of them, and a new cohort's lesson 4 is half player-made. A canon archetype with a sworn Term is a vow three people took in one hall, carried by strangers, which is the lineage system working at the level of the server.
