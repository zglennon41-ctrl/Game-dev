# Characters and Slots

A character is a life: one blood, one Shape, one genius, one name the world remembers. An account has three of them per server, and the rules below exist so that three lives feel like three people and not one person with three bodies.

## What a slot is

A slot is a character on one server. The three slots are independent in everything that is personal: blood, Shape, genius, Stages, archetypes, Marks, gear, Favor, and story flags. They share only the account's Notes (money is an account wallet per server) and the account's Chronicle entry, the record of what all three have done.

| Shared across the account's three characters | Separate per character |
| --- | --- |
| Notes (the server wallet) | Blood, Shape, Seed and genius |
| Chronicle record and account standing with the Compact | Stages, Patterns, sworn Terms |
| Known archetype canon (what the Archive shows you) | Marks, ranks, builds |
| Cosmetics bought for the account | Favor with each house |
| Friends, blocks, chat | Quests completed, story flags, deaths witnessed |

## Creation

Creation is the first hour, and the Academy enrollment desk is where it happens in fiction. The order matters: blood first, because everything else is read from it.

1. **Blood.** Pick the tier: Mark, Kindred, or Bloodline. A Mark is chosen from a list of forty; a Kindred from the twelve of the launch region; a Bloodline from six, which the Academy registers on the spot and the Compact will want a writ for.
2. **City of origin.** One of the three reachable at launch (Columbia, Cahokia, Lunden). It sets your first Discipline, your starting gear genre, your accent, and which house knows your name.
3. **Genius.** Choose a Seed, a one-line leaning for what will stand beside you. The genius's first form is read at the rite from the Seed, your blood, and your Shape, and every form after is read from how you actually play.
4. **Appearance.** Blood is always visible; the rest is yours. The guild mark's placement is chosen later, at Commencement.
5. **Name.** Unique per server. The Chronicle will use it.
6. **Sworn or not.** The one irreversible choice, below.

The rite that finds your Shape happens in the first Academy week, not at creation, and you do not choose it. You can retake the rite once per character, in the Writ season, at the cost of every Mark earned so far.

## The second and third character

Second and third characters enter the Academy with advanced standing (the Academy tab covers it) and graduate into a cohort like anyone else. They may join the first character's guild. The rules that keep an account from becoming a guild:

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| One officer seat per account, across all three characters | Three alts cannot fill a council |
| In a Commons-charter guild the account votes once, whichever character is logged in | One account, one vote |
| An account's characters cannot be in two guilds that face each other in the same Rally season; the second is flagged kin and sits out | No scouting your own match |
| An account's three characters are never in one party; a party holds three accounts | A party is three people |
| Founding needs three graduates from three different accounts, or NPC classmates to fill | A guild is not founded alone |

Alts in the same guild do get something: the hearth treats them as kin. A shade accepted by your first character accepts the second at half the usual wait.

## Retirement to the hearth

A character can retire. Retirement is permanent, frees the slot, and places the character's genius at their guild's hearth as a shade, where it teaches that character's Patterns to members it accepts. The character's name stays in the Chronicle with the word retired. A retired character's sworn Terms are released without breach.

Retirement needs a guild (a Company without a hall retires to the Academy hearth instead), a genius at its Fourth Form or beyond, and at least one Pattern. A First-Form shade fades in a week.

## Sworn characters

At creation a character may be made Sworn. A Sworn character can die, permanently: downed past the sixty seconds with no revive, or felled by a sworn Term's breach. In exchange, a Sworn character earns double Marks, can swear a fourth Term, and leaves a shade on death that keeps every shadow its genius carried. Sworn is shown on the character; the board shows it on the party. A Sworn character cannot be made unsworn.

Sworn is the hardcore flag and the lineage engine at once: the characters a server remembers best are the ones who could die, and did.

## Legacy

When a character retires or dies, three things persist: the shade, the archetypes they founded (the founder's name stays on them forever), and the Chronicle. A new character in the same slot inherits none of it mechanically and all of it socially, because the hall remembers who you were.

## Edge cases

- Deleting an unretired character below Stage 3 is free and leaves nothing. Above Stage 3 it is retirement without the shade, and the game says so twice.
- A character cannot be transferred between servers; servers are different worlds.
- A character in a dissolved guild keeps everything personal and is a Company of one until Commencement, where they may be recruited again.
- Name changes are allowed once, by Compact petition, and the Chronicle records both names.
