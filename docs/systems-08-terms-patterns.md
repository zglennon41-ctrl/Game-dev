# Terms and Patterns

A Pattern is a technique that is yours, and a Term is the condition that makes it strong. The grammar is fixed, 55 Terms in three kinds, so every Pattern reads as a sentence and the game can judge every breach; the combinations are in the thousands, and the Terms a character picks are the most private thing about them.

## Building a Pattern

At Stage 4 a character builds a Pattern at a hearth. The builder is a sentence with blanks.

1. **Form.** One technique from the character's own Shape, any family. This is what the Pattern does.
2. **Terms.** Up to three, at most one of each kind: a Trigger, a Toll, a Bar. A Sworn character may take a fourth Term of any kind.
3. **Name.** Public. Everyone at the hearth hears it; the Terms, nobody.
4. **Swear or set.** Set it and it holds; swear it before three witnesses and the Hearthkeeper, and it doubles.

The Pattern is a chain on the pulse: three beats for the Form, plus one beat per Term, to six; a Duet with a genius at its Fourth Form or beyond doubles the chain, and Resonance adds verses (see the coupling below). Hit every beat and the full multiplier pays; miss one and the Pattern fizzles, the Will is spent, and the Toll is still taken. A character has one Pattern at a time and may rebuild it at the Thaw.

## Multipliers

| Term kind | Multiplier | Can it be breached |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trigger | ×1.5 | No: if the Trigger is not met, the Pattern is simply unavailable |
| Toll | ×2 | No: the Toll is taken automatically |
| Bar | ×1.5 | Yes: a Bar crossed is a breach, and the Bar is the risk |
| Sworn | ×2 on the whole | A sworn breach is permanent |

Stage 4 output is ×4 on the Pattern's Form; three Terms sworn reach ×9 on top of that. Nothing else in the game reaches ×9, which is why a Master with a sworn Pattern is a different kind of thing in a zone.

## Swearing

Swearing happens at a hearth on a Sunday, before three witnesses who are members (players or NPC) and the Hearthkeeper. The sworn Terms are recorded in the hearth, not the Archive: the hall knows, the world does not. A sworn Pattern doubles. A sworn breach loses the Pattern permanently, costs the hall 5 Atmosphere, and every shade at that hearth knows; some will not speak to the character again. A Sworn character's sworn breach is death. An unsworn breach loses the Pattern for a season.

The game judges breaches, not players: a Bar is a rule the engine checks on every use, and the Pattern refuses to fire against a foe the Bar covers unless the player overrides the warning, which is the breach.

## Reaching into the Gap

Every blood has a Gap, a thing the line cannot do. A Pattern can reach into the Gap only if it is sworn and carries a Toll, and the Form is drawn from the Gap's own family. The dragon-blooded who swore never to fly can breathe cold. This is the only route around the Gap, and it is the one most of the server's legends take.

## Field and intention: the coupling

The Pattern is the field's intention and the genius is the field itself, and the two are bound by Resonance, defined in full on the Genius tab. In short: the Pattern earns Intention tiers by witnessed use (I built, II at 50, III at 150, IV at 400, V sworn with Master rank), each tier adds a verse to the chain, and the Pattern pays at Resonance, the higher of its tier and the genius's instar. A character who bonds, witnesses, and evolves their genius to the Fifth Form has a Pattern with two verses even if they rarely fire it; a character who fires it four hundred times has a genius that fights like a Fifth Form even if it has not evolved. An evolution grants the next tier's verse; a tier gained unlocks the next Reading. Neither side loses the power of the side the player neglected, and the genius of a Pattern-heavy character is read from the Pattern's Terms, so it comes to look like the intention it grew beside.

## The Pattern as identity

The name is public and the Terms are not, so the hall's culture is guessing. A member who learns another's Terms by watching has a hold on them, and using a Bar against someone (forcing the foe they cannot strike into their path) is the one act the hall will not forgive. Terms are also the ground of founding: three members with the same Form and Terms make an archetype, which is why a founding is a shared vow.
