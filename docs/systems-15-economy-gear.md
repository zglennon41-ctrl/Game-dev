# Economy and Gear

The economy has four currencies and nothing that can be bought with real money except cosmetics. Notes are money, Favor is a house's regard, Standing is a guild's score, and Marks are a character's record; none converts into another, and the things that matter most (Terms, Patterns, shades, canon) are not for sale at all.

## The four currencies

| Currency | Held by | Earned by | Spent on | Converts to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notes | The account, per server | Quests, loot sold, tenancy received, market trade | Gear, rooms, consumables, tenancy paid, tolls, the Smith | Nothing |
| Favor | Each character, per house | Writs and Escorts for the house | Writs of passage, registration, Ledger weight, house gear | Nothing |
| Standing | Each guild | Linework, Rallies, crisis work, relit junctions; decays 10% a season | The Allotment, tier, Convention seats; a Toll can burn a point | Nothing |
| Marks | Each character, per archetype | Witnessed quests | Ranks; a Toll can burn one | Nothing |

## Where Notes come from and go

Notes enter through quest pay (set per type and color), loot sold at the Outfitter, and the Saturday market, and leave through gear, hall rooms, consumables, tenancy, and Compact tolls. The guild treasury takes a cut of every member's quest pay (set by the Charter, 10% default) and pays for rooms, tenancy, and the Rally stake. Sinks are tuned so that a Chartered guild of twenty active players can build one room a month and a Great guild two.

## Gear tiers

Gear is tiered by line color, and the tier is a gate on where it drops, not on who can wear it.

| Tier | Drops on | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | Green | A weapon or piece with one property |
| Marked | Amber | Two properties; one from the zone's territory |
| Named | Red | Three properties and a name; the Chronicle records who found it |
| Quiet | Black | A property no Smith can make; carried out only by a character who used no Pattern in the Quiet |
| House | Bought with Favor | A property tied to the house's blood; Calvert gear is light, Carroll gear runs hot |

## City genre gear

Every piece of gear is a city's genre: Columbia's leather and brass, Cahokia's oilskin and bone, Lunden's wool and fog-glass, Colonia's iron and gauge. Genre is cosmetic and tempo: a Colonia coat is heavy and slows the pulse by a beat in ten; a Lunden coat is light and quickens it. Gear from a city whose route is not open comes only by caravan or Named drop, which is why a Jiangnan sash on a year-one character is a story.

## The Smith

The Smith is a hall room. It reforges (move a property between two pieces of the same tier), tempers (add a territory property from materials), and names (promote a Marked piece to Named with a Red-zone material and a witness). It cannot make Quiet properties or house properties. Smithing takes real hours (reforge 1, temper 6, name 24), collected at the lamp hour.

## Trade

Players trade through the hall board and the Saturday market at the Mountain Dock: direct trade of gear and materials for Notes, no auction house, no cross-server trade. Named gear carries its finder's name through every trade. A guild may post a buy order on its board for materials it needs for a room. Caravans do not carry gear.

## Consumables

Field-salt (restores Will, Apothecary), lamp-oil (lights a dark area for a run), bindings (a revive without standing three beats, once a run), and the Compact's writs of passage. Consumables are the only thing the Apothecary sells, and the Apothecary is the only shop that stocks them.

## What is never for sale

| Never bought | Only earned by |
| --- | --- |
| A Stage | The Academy and the hearth |
| A Term or a Pattern | Swearing and building |
| A shade's teaching | Acceptance |
| Canon | Founding and use |
| A junction | The Allotment, the Rally, or the Convention |
| Standing | Work |
| Favor | Writs |
| A Mark | A witness |

Real money buys cosmetics only: coats, marks' styling, genius colors, hall decorations. Nothing real-money changes the pulse, the Terms, or the Chronicle.
