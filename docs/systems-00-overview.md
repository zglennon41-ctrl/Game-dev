# WILDLINE — Systems

Nov 4, 2026 · @Zak

*Fifteen systems, one tab each, for a game where every character passes through the Academy, every guild is founded by classmates, the calendar runs on real time, and after three years no two servers are the same.*

## What this is

This is the systems layer under the WILDLINE design bible: fifteen tabs, one per system, each written so a team could build it from the tab alone. The bible says what the game is; this says how each part works, with numbers, edge cases, and open questions.

Four things changed from the bible, and they run through every tab:

1. **Three character slots per server.** Every character is a separate life with its own blood, Shape, and genius. Second and third characters may join the first character's guild, under rules that keep one account from owning a guild.
2. **Everyone goes through the Academy.** The Academy at Alexandria is the first act of every character, first or third. You graduate with a cohort, and you either join a guild that recruits at Commencement or found one with your classmates.
3. **The calendar is real time.** One real day is one game day. The week, the month, and the four seasons all land on real dates, and each season carries a crisis storyline whose outcome the whole server decides.
4. **Servers diverge.** Every outcome, founded archetype, lit junction, risen guild, and dead house writes into the server's Chronicle. After a three-year Charter term, a new character on one server is in a different world from a new character on another.

The systems feed each other in one direction: the Academy makes characters, characters make guilds, guilds run quests, quests decide crises, crises write history, and history is the world the next cohort graduates into. The logic systems (Shapes, Terms, Blood, Genius, Archetypes) sit under all of it and feed Combat.

## The constants

Every tab uses these numbers. Change one here and it changes everywhere.

| Constant | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Time | One real day is one game day; the day turns at 04:00 server time (the lamp hour) |
| Seasons | Thaw (Mar 20), Writ (Jun 21), Rally (Sep 22), Dark (Dec 21); 13 weeks each; one crisis per season |
| Charter term | Three years, twelve crises; the third Dark ends in the Charter Convention |
| Slots | Three characters per account per server |
| Academy | 8–12 hours for a first character, 3–4 with advanced standing; a cohort is one week's enrollments plus 12 NPC classmates |
| Founding | Three graduates of one cohort, players or NPC classmates |
| Guild | 60 player characters and 30 NPC members; a Master and four officers; one officer seat per account |
| Junctions | 48 per server; 12 lit at launch by elder guilds; the rest dark, awarded at the Thaw Allotment |
| Guild tiers | Company, Chartered, Great, Seat |
| Shapes and Stages | 6 Shapes in 3 pairs; 4 Stages plus Still and Fold |
| Terms | 20 Triggers, 20 Tolls, 15 Bars; ×1.5, ×2, ×1.5; sworn doubles; ×9 is the ceiling |
| Blood | 3 tiers; 6 bloodlines; 12 kindreds at launch, 120 across the cities |
| Genius | Six instars read from play at milestones (minimum days 0, 7, 30, 90, 365, 540); up to 3 secondary forms; Resonance couples genius and Pattern |
| Archetypes | 72 base; founding needs 3 members with 10 witnessed uses each; canonized at the season turn |
| Party | 3 characters and 3 geniuses; excursions 20–40 minutes; 4 line colors |
| Board | Refreshes at the lamp hour; 9–15 postings per hall per day |
| Currencies | Notes (money), Favor (per house), Standing (per guild), Marks (per character per archetype) |
| Server | 6,000–10,000 active accounts; 150–300 guilds |
