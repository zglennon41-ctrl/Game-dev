# Blood

Blood is what a character was born carrying: a Mark, a kindred, or a bloodline. It sets the raw material, the Gap, how the rite leans, how the cities treat you, and what shows on your body. Blood is chosen at creation and never changes.

## The three tiers

| Tier | What it is | Gives | Gap | Rite lean | Cities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mark | One mutation, yours alone | A passive bonus that foretells the Pattern | Small: a weakness to one Shape | None | Ignored |
| Kindred | A congenital set shared by a people | A Gift the people share, plus the people's culture and kin at the hall | Moderate: a thing the people cannot do | Toward the people's Shape, 40% | Known; some kindreds are watched |
| Bloodline | Strong blood, six lines | A Trunk of three techniques no one else can learn, and a visible body | Large: a thing the line can never do | Toward the line's Shape, 70% | Registered; a writ needed above Green unless the server decided otherwise |

## Marks

Forty at launch. A Mark is a sentence about the body and a hint about the Pattern.

| Mark | Bonus | Foretells |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eyes that see heat | Read techniques find hidden foes | A Read Pattern |
| A second heart | Survive one fall per run at 1 health | An Anchor Pattern |
| A voice beasts obey | One beast per run hesitates on a shout | A Bind Pattern |
| Reads currents | Will regenerates 50% faster near water | A Bind or Read Pattern |
| Iron in the blood | Make constructs last two beats longer | A Make Pattern |
| Never cold | Immune to cold; weak to heat | A Turn Pattern |
| Long stride | Cast Speed techniques cost 25% less | A Cast Pattern |
| The hearth hears you | Shades accept you a season sooner | Any Pattern; the Hearthkeeper's Mark |

## The six bloodlines

| Line | House | Shows as | Trunk techniques | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Winged | Calvert | Wings, folded or not | Lift (a beat of flight), Stoop (a diving strike), Updraft (carry a member) | Cannot hold a post: Anchor Wall at 40%; wings break in the Quiet |
| Dragon-blooded | Carroll | Scales at the throat and hands, slit eyes | Breath (fire), Hoard-sense (see Notes and gear through walls), Scale (a Fold that lasts six beats) | Cannot be cold; no Turn cold, ever |
| Shapechanger | Howard | Nothing, which is the point; the eyes give it away to Read | Second Face (wear a living person's face a day), Hide (Still with the genius inside you), Unmake (drop a Bind) | Cannot swear: their Patterns are never sworn, so never ×2 |
| Demon-blooded | Key | Horns, a tail, a voice with two notes | Contract (a Bind that binds both), Night Step (a Cast blink in shadow), Name (learn a foe's Bar by striking) | Cannot be Mended by another; only their own Read |
| Demigod | Banneker | A glow under the skin at night; too tall | Oracle (Foresight three beats), Weight of Word (a Bind by speech), Ascent (a Fold of the whole field, once a run) | Cannot Still; the field never closes |
| Fused | None; the hall takes them | A body that is also a beast: fur, claws, a wolf's gait | The beast's Trunk, by base form | No separate genius: no shade, no Duet, no shadows, and they cannot hear the hearth |

Bloodline characters start with the Trunk's first technique and earn the other two by Marks. A house claims its line: a Winged character has House Calvert's attention from day one, which is Favor and danger in equal parts.

## Registration and writs

The cities register strong blood, and the Compact enforces the registry on the Lines: a registered character needs a writ to run above Green. Writs are bought with Favor from any house, issued by a Chartered guild under the guild exemption if the server decided that in crisis 5, or forged, which the Compact treats as a Writ-war offense. On a server where the Registration was resisted, bloodlines run free and the cities' Writs pay less to guilds that shelter them.

## Children and lineage

Player characters do not have children; NPC members do, and a child's blood follows the parents: two kindreds of one people breed true, a bloodline and anyone yields the line one time in three, and a Mark is a coin flip. Children grow up in the hall over a real year and enroll in the Academy at the Thaw after, in a cohort with players, which is how a guild raises its own.
