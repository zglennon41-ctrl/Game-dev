# The Genius: how it changes

A genius changes in five ways, and each one answers a different question: growth answers what have you become, adaptation answers what have you been doing lately, specialization answers what do you want it for, hybridization answers who have you been beside, and form change answers what did the world do to you. The anatomy comes first, because every mechanism is a change to one of its parts.

## Anatomy

A genius is a silhouette wearing features, in a Shape's palette. Everything visible on it is a part with a rule.

| Part | Count | What it is | Comes from | Persists |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Silhouette | 1 | The body plan: one of six per Expression, plus twelve hybrid silhouettes | The Reading | Until the next instar |
| Expression | 1 | Beast, Kith, Engine, Relic, Daemon, or Aspect | The Reading | Until a Reading crosses it |
| Seed-mark | 1 | The feature the Seed promised | Creation | Always |
| Heritage features | 1–3 | Parts carried from the last form | The Reading, chosen from the old form's features and adaptations | Until replaced at a later Reading |
| Adaptations | 0–2 | Small parts grown from recent play | The Tally, between Readings | Fade after a season without the play; can be fixed as heritage |
| Grafts | 0–3 | Parts taken from other fields | Hybridization | Always |
| Specialist feature | 0–1 | The part the Focus adds | Specialization | While the Focus holds |
| Palette | 1 | Shape color and proportion: Anchor broad, Cast long, Read luminous | The rite | Always; intensifies by instar |
| Secondary forms | 0–3 | Shifts the genius can take | Conditions met | Persist; a form that no longer fits the Expression goes vestigial at half effect |
| Shadows | 0–3 | Witnessed Patterns it can use | Witness | Until forgotten |

A Sixth-Form genius can wear up to nine features around one silhouette, and all nine have a sentence behind them.

## The five mechanisms

| Mechanism | Answers | Trigger | What changes | Read or chosen | Reverses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Growth | What have you become | An instar milestone | Silhouette, possibly Expression, the set of heritage features, palette intensity | Read: three candidates, player picks or settles | Once per life, by Reversion |
| Adaptation | What have you been doing | Tally thresholds between Readings | One or two small features with small bonuses | Read, automatic | Fades in a season without the play |
| Specialization | What do you want it for | The Fourth Form; then each Thaw | Focus: output tilts to one family or role; a specialist feature; secondary forms | Chosen | At the Thaw |
| Hybridization | Who have you been beside | A hundred runs beside one field, a named beast, a shade, your own blood, a crisis | A graft; possibly a hybrid silhouette | Offered at a Reading, player accepts; a crisis graft is forced | Never |
| Form change | What did the world do to you | Shift, the Quiet, three falls, Union, Reversion, the hearth | The whole form for a time, a secondary form lost, a forced Reading, the final form | By the event | Depends on the event |

## 1. Growth

Growth is the instar system: six forms over a life, unlocked by milestones, each one a Reading. The Reading scores every form in the lattice against the Tally and offers three. Each candidate shows its Expression, which of the current form's features and adaptations it would carry as heritage (one to three), and the lines of the Tally that chose it. The player picks one or lets it settle on the best fit. Growth is the only mechanism that changes the silhouette, and it is irreversible except once: at a Thaw, a character may petition their hearth for Reversion, which returns the genius to its previous form with its grafts and Seed-mark intact and its newest secondary form lost. Reversion is once per life and the shades talk about it.

What the Reading weighs, in order of weight: the Pattern's Form and Terms (intention), range and technique families (how), company and ground (where and with whom), orders (what the genius was asked for), bonds and the hall (who it belongs to), blood (what it came from). A Reading can cross Expressions when two or more of the top lines point away from the current kind.

## 2. Adaptation

Between Readings the genius drifts. The Tally keeps running thresholds, and when one is crossed the genius grows an adaptation: a small, visible feature with a small bonus, in one of two slots. Adaptations are the genius's short memory, and they fade after a season without the play that made them.

| Adaptation | Threshold | Feature | Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Webbed | 30 runs in water zones | Webbing, fins, a sheen | Will regen in water |
| Dark-eyed | 30 runs after dark | Wide eyes, dark coat | Sees a shade further on Black lines |
| Long-legged | 200 Cast Speed uses in its field | Longer limbs | Hunt reaches a beat sooner |
| Plated | 100 runs beside a Make | Shell or plate on the shoulders | Takes one more hit on Guard |
| Set feet | 50 fights at close range | Heavier stance, broader base | Hold is not pushed by third molts |
| Hall-fat | A season of kitchen Atmosphere above 70 | Sleek, heavier, content | Falls recover a day sooner |
| Scorched | 100 Turn fire uses in its field | Singed edges, ember eyes | Resists fire |
| Tunnel-ear | 50 Linework runs | Larger ears, a listening tilt | Hears a post's break before Read does |

An adaptation present at a Reading can be fixed: chosen as heritage, it becomes a permanent feature of the next form. This is the hinge between the short memory and the long one, and it is how a player's habits become their genius's body.

## 3. Specialization

At the Fourth Form the player chooses a Focus for the first time, and may change it each Thaw. A Focus tilts the genius toward one thing and adds a specialist feature that shows what it is for.

| Focus | Tilts | Specialist feature | Costs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warden | Guard: blocks one more hit, Pressure resistance | A broad brow, a raised guard | Hunt output −10% |
| Hunter | Hunt: its family techniques at ×1.25 | Longer claws, a forward lean | Guard blocks one fewer |
| Post | Hold: holds a post alone; the party regroups inside its field | Roots, a wide base, a lantern | Cannot Hunt while holding |
| Voice | Free and Duet: the Duet chain gains a beat | A visible voice: a throat-light, a second mouth | Guard −10% |
| Mirror | Shadows: cap +1; shadows at ×1.5 | A reflective surface somewhere on it | No specialist bonus elsewhere |

Secondary forms are specialization's other half: shifts unlocked by conditions, up to three, each a different job for the same body. The Systems doc's Genius tab lists the ten at launch.

## 4. Hybridization

Fields that overlap long enough leave something in each other. A graft is a feature taken from another field, offered at the next Reading, accepted or declined, and permanent once taken. Three grafts at most, and two grafts from another Expression let the Reading offer a hybrid silhouette: a Beast with Engine joints, a Kith with a Daemon's horns.

| Source | Condition | The graft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Another player's genius | 100 runs together, both present | A Kin-form: each genius takes one feature of the other; the pair's geniuses gain Chorus, a Duet both can join when both characters are in the party |
| A named beast | The same named Hunt beast killed five times | One of its features; its Shape's resistance |
| A shade | The shade's acceptance | A feature of the dead genius; the shade's one Pattern as a shadow that does not count against the cap |
| Your own blood | A bloodline character at the Third Form or beyond | The line's feature: scales, horns, a glow, a second face |
| A crisis | An ending that says so (the Unfounded spread) | A graft nobody chose, on every genius that witnessed it |

Kin-forms are the social system inside the companion system. Two players who have run a hundred lines together have geniuses that look related and fight as a pair, and the hall can see it.

## 5. Form change

The world changes a genius's form in six ways that are not growth.

| Event | What happens | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shift | The genius takes a secondary form | Until shifted back, or the run ends |
| The Quiet | A genius that enters the Quiet and comes out takes a forced Reading with the Quiet feature (a place on it with no field) among the candidates' heritage | The next instar is Quiet-touched |
| Three falls | Loses its newest secondary form | Until the condition is met again |
| Union | Merges with the character for seven beats: the fused's body, every shadow available | Seven beats, once a run |
| Reversion | Returns to the previous form | Permanent, once per life |
| The hearth | Becomes a shade in its final form | Always |

## Rules of carryover

1. The Seed-mark is always carried.
2. Heritage is one to three features chosen at the Reading from the old form's features and adaptations; the candidate shows which it would keep.
3. Grafts are always carried, and a hybrid silhouette needs two from one other Expression.
4. Adaptations not fixed as heritage are lost at the Reading.
5. Secondary forms persist; one that no longer fits the Expression goes vestigial at half effect, and a later Reading can shed it.
6. The palette never changes; it deepens.
7. A shade keeps everything.

## A full lineage

Mira seeds something that watches from above, carries the Mark long stride, and the bowl gives her Cast. Her First Form is a hawk.

| Instar | Form | Expression | Carried | New | What read it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hawk | Beast | Seed-mark: far-eyes | Wings, talons | Seed, Cast, long stride |
| 2, the Gate | Stooping Kith | Kith | Far-eyes; wings as a cloak | A short blade; the adaptation set feet, fixed | Close fighting with a saber; Guard orders; the exam party's Anchor |
| 3, the Pattern | Hawk-Mask | Relic (worn) | Far-eyes; set feet | The mask; the Kin-form horn-brow from Oona's ram | The Pattern Stoop (Cast Reach, Trigger after a perfect Hold); Marshal Discipline; 100 runs beside Oona |
| 4, Adept Draw-Cutter | Hawk-Mask, Edged | Relic | Far-eyes; set feet; horn-brow | Focus: Warden; secondary form Arms | Edo blade tempo; Hunts; Guard |
| 5, Master | Falcon-Harness | Relic–Engine hybrid | Far-eyes; set feet; horn-brow; a Quiet place from the Reef | Clockwork wings (graft from a Colonia Engine shade); Storm | The Reef run; the shade's acceptance; Turn fire from a party-mate |

Nothing in that table was chosen from a menu except the Seed, three Readings out of three candidates, a Focus, and two grafts accepted. Everything else is what Mira did.
