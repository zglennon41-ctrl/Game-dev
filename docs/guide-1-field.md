# The Field

A field is the pressure a living will puts on the world, and it has seven measurable properties, seven states it can be in, and a small set of ways it interacts with other fields. Every rule in the game about magic is a rule about one of these.

## The seven properties

| Property | What it is | Base | Changes by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reserve (Will) | How much the field can spend before it is empty | 100 | Never; what a point buys changes instead |
| Reach | How far past the skin the field extends | The skin | Stage: skin, 2 paces, 10 paces, 10 paces and a Pattern's throw |
| Density | The field's output multiplier | ×1 | Stage: ×1, ×2, ×3, ×4; Fold concentrates it further for three beats |
| Shape | The direction the field bends | One of six, read at the rite | Never, except one retake of the rite |
| Pulse | The field's tempo; the beat window | Set by Shape, modified by gear | Weapon class and coat weight; a Chord syncs it to the party |
| Leak | Field spilling past control | Full leak untrained | Hold stops it; exhaustion and some Tolls restore it |
| Pressure | What the field does to other fields nearby by existing | None | Bloodlines (a demigod's weight, a demon's two-note voice), Apex geniuses, fourth-molt beasts |

Reserve regenerates at 4 a second with the field open and 0 when Still. A perfect Hold refunds 10. Stances cost 2 a second; techniques 8 to 25; a Pattern 30 plus its Toll. At 0 the field faints: no techniques, no stance, the pulse slows by half, and the character leaks until Will is back to 20.

## The seven states

A field is always in exactly one state, and the state is visible on the pulse ring.

| State | Entered by | The field | Leaves by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open | Default | Regenerating, leaking only if untrained | Any input |
| Held | Guard | Contained; a parry on the beat | Release |
| Shaped | Stance toggle | Carrying the Shape's quality; 2 Will a second | Toggle, or Will at 0 |
| Projected | A technique | Extended past the skin for the technique's beats | The technique ends |
| Patterned | The Pattern button | The chain; every beat judged; Terms in force | The chain ends or fizzles |
| Still | Guard held with no foe locked | Closed: invisible to beasts and Read, no regeneration, no genius | Any input, or a Pattern at tier IV |
| Folded | Attack held | All density in one part for up to three beats; the rest leaks for three after | Three beats, or the strike |

Still and Fold are the two states the Academy does not teach; they come from a Varanasi Ascetic, an Edo Blade, an Anchor Master, or a shade.

## How fields interact

**Party.** Three fields in one zone see each other's pulses. When all three land perfect inputs on the same beat eight times in a row, the party enters Chord: for the next three beats every input is perfect, Patterns pay double, and the genius orders cost nothing. Chord is the ensemble reward and the reason the car's briefing is about tempo.

**Clash.** Two fields of opposite Shapes pressing on each other interfere: a Bind leash on a Turn-shaped foe slips, a Turn element on a Bind-shaped foe takes. The wheel's affinity table is the interference table, and the three-break stagger is what a clash looks like when it goes one way.

**Halls.** A hall's field is the sum of its members' fields, held in a shape by the hearth. It keeps the wild out of the tracks, it is what shades live in, and its strength is the roster's Shapes summed with a bonus for variety, which is why a hall of twelve Anchors is weaker than a hall of six Shapes. A hall's field has a reach of its junction's tunnels; past it the line is wild.

**The wild's weather.** Between the halls, fields are unstable, and zones have weather of will: gusts (every field's density doubles for ten beats), lulls (regeneration stops), crosswinds (pulses drift off tempo), and fronts (the Shape the zone favors shifts). Weather is read on the car's instruments before the party detrains and is part of the posting.

**Beasts.** A beast's field is its Shape and its molt. First molts leak; fourth molts have Pressure, which pushes a player's pulse off beat within five paces unless they are Held or Still. Beasts find leaking fields at twice the distance of contained ones.

**The Quiet.** The place with no field. Inside it: no regeneration, no pulse (every input is off-beat unless the character goes Still and counts), geniuses silent, Read blind, Pressure gone. The Quiet is where the old world is, and the rule for it is that the field has nothing to push against.

**Resonance posts.** Anchors of a hall's field placed along a line. A post is held by a Make Ward or an Anchor standing three beats; a held post is a checkpoint, a half-restore, and the thing that keeps the line lit after the party has gone home.

## Damage to a field

A field can be hurt in three ways that are not health.

| Harm | Cause | Effect | Heals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bruise | A Bind Contract, a Pressure foe, a Toll like leaking | Reach reduced one step for the run; the pulse widens | At the hearth at the lamp hour |
| Scar | A sworn breach; a Sworn death witnessed by the genius | Reach reduced one step for a season; visible on the ring | A season; a shade's forgiveness ends it early |
| Tear | The Quiet, left while Patterned | The field cannot be Patterned until mended by a Read Master or a Varanasi Ascetic | Mending, a real quest |

## How a field grows

The field grows vertically by Stage, four times, and never again. After Stage 4 it grows in what it can be made to do (techniques), in what it is remembered for (archetypes), in what it has vowed (the Pattern's tiers), and in what it has become beside the character (the genius's instars). Resonance makes the last two one number: the higher of the Pattern's tier and the genius's instar is the level both fight at.

| Growth | Kind | Cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stage | Vertical: density and reach | 4 |
| Techniques | Breadth: what the field can do | 162 Shape techniques plus Disciplines and Trunks |
| Grades | Depth: how well it does each | Perfected at 500 uses |
| Archetypes | Recognition: what the world calls it | Master at 50 Marks |
| Intention | The Pattern's growth | Tier V |
| Instar | The genius's growth | Apex |
