# Skills to Jobs

Skills belong to the character and jobs belong to the world. A technique is one thing a field can be made to do; the character learns it from someone, grades it by use, and keeps it forever. An archetype is a named bundle of techniques the world recognizes, and the character earns the name by witnessed work. The job system is the layer where private skill becomes public identity.

## Six kinds of skill

| Kind | What it is | How many | Who has it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shape technique | A technique from one of the Shape's three families | 162 at launch, 27 per Shape | Anyone, at the wheel's affinity |
| Discipline technique | A city school's technique, tied to its weapon class | 3 per Discipline, 36 | Anyone in the school |
| Trunk technique | A bloodline's | 3 per line, 18 | That blood only |
| Mastery technique | An archetype's capstone | 1 per archetype | Masters of it, and those a Master teaches |
| Pattern | The character's signature | 1 per character | The character |
| Shadow | A witnessed Pattern carried by the genius | Up to 3 | The genius |

A technique has a cost in Will, a number of beats, a family, and a grade.

## Where techniques come from

| Source | Teaches | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Academy | Hold, Shape, the first family's first three, the city Discipline's first | Enrollment |
| An Instructor | Any technique on the Academy's curriculum, which on an old server includes founded archetypes | A seat in the lesson |
| A master in the yard | Any technique the master holds, at the master's price | The master's time; a Rally yard has visiting masters |
| A shade | The dead's Patterns and the techniques the dead held | Acceptance |
| The Archive | An archetype's techniques by rank | Marks |
| Salvage fragments | A technique assembled from three fragments found in ruins | The fragments; the Archive assembles |
| A Kin-form | Chorus, the paired Duet | A hundred runs together |

## Grades

Every technique has three grades by use, and the grade goes with the technique wherever the character takes it.

| Grade | Uses | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learned | 0 | Base output; the full Will cost |
| Practiced | 100 | Output ×1.2; one beat shorter if it was three or more |
| Perfected | 500 | Output ×1.5; Will cost −25%; the technique can be used from Still if it is a Hold or Read technique |

Grades are the depth axis. A three-year character with thirty perfected techniques is stronger than a one-year character with the same list, and nothing but use gets them there.

## Families and affinity

Each Shape has three families (the Field tab names them); a character uses their own Shape's at full output, the two neighbors' at 80%, the two beyond at 60%, the opposite at 40%, and the wheel caps how much of a foreign family can be learned: all of a neighbor's, two of a two-away's three, one technique per family of the opposite. A Pattern is never built from a foreign family.

## Disciplines

A Discipline is how a city teaches. It has a weapon class, a stance modifier, three school techniques shared by all six of its archetypes, and a rite of the school to enter.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marshal | Musket, pistol, saber | The shot: the first Project of a fight is perfect | Covering Fire, Warrant, Last Round | Serve a Writ |
| Pilot | Harpoon, hook, oar-staff | Footing: no stagger on moving ground | Sounding, Line Across, Capstan | A river crossing |

## How an archetype is composed

An archetype is Shape × Discipline, and its nine techniques are composed by one rule so that every cell on the grid is legible.

| Slots | Drawn from |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 (Journeyman) | Three from the Shape's first family |
| 4–5 (Adept) | Two from the second family |
| 6 (Adept) | One from the third family |
| 7–9 (Master) | The three Discipline techniques |
| Mastery | One capstone, unique to the archetype |

A Deadeye (Cast Marshal) is three Shot techniques, two Reach, one Speed, the Marshal's three, and a capstone, Dead Reckoning. A Draw-Cutter (Cast Blade) shares the six Cast techniques and has the Blade's three instead, with Iaijutsu as its capstone. That shared core is why switching between archetypes of one Shape is cheap and switching Shapes is a life.

## Ranks

| Rank | Needs | Gets | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Journeyman | Stage 3; take it at an Archive; the rite of the school | Stance; slots 1–3 | Nothing yet |
| Adept | 10 Marks in it; Stage 4 | Slots 4–6; gear affinity | The archetype's name on the character |
| Master | 50 Marks and a Pattern built in it | Slots 7–9; the mastery technique; may teach; may take an Instructor seat | The name in the Chronicle |

A Mark is a quest completed with the archetype equipped and a party member present. Green 1, Amber 2, Red 3, Black 5; Sworn ×2; Archivists ×1.25.

## The build

Eight slots: the Pattern, four techniques from any archetype held, two stances, and the Duet. A build is set at the hall or in the car. Techniques come with their grades; a stance is an archetype's and carries its Discipline modifier. Two stances means two Disciplines' modifiers in one fight at the cost of a beat to switch.

## Switching and crosses

Switching archetypes at the hall is free; techniques learned stay learned. A character Adept in two archetypes holds a cross title, shown as both names (a Deadeye Draw-Cutter), and a cross across two Shapes is rare enough that the Chronicle records it. Master in two archetypes of one Discipline earns the school's own title (a Marshal of Columbia), which the city's houses notice.

## Founding

A Pattern used by three members of one guild, with the identical Form and Terms, ten witnessed uses each, becomes a provisional archetype; the Systems doc's Archetypes tab has the canon rules. What founding does to the skill layer: the founded archetype's nine techniques are composed by the same rule from the founders' shared build, so a founded job is legible to a stranger on day one, and its mastery technique is the Pattern itself, which the taker must swear if the founders swore.

## The genius's skills

The genius has its own list: its family's techniques (the Shape variant's family, on Hunt), its shadows, its secondary forms, and Chorus if it has a Kin-form. The genius's techniques grade by the same use rule, and a Perfected genius technique is the one thing a player cannot buy, teach, or transfer.
